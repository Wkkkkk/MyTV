## Incident

**Live-status badge `yt-dlp` OOM (06-10).** Lazy live-status badges fanned out enough concurrent `yt-dlp` subprocesses (~73 MB each) to OOM the 256 MB production VM. Resolved with a global 2-permit semaphore, acquiring the permit *before* starting the timeout clock, and bounding the permit wait to load-shed rather than park. Timed-out processes are now killed rather than orphaned. Five duplicated invocation bodies folded into one capped helper. Logged in `docs/bug-logs/2026-06-10-live-status-badge-ytdlp-oom.md`.
