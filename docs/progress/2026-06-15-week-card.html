Incident · 06-10
Live-status badge yt-dlp OOM
Lazy live-status badges fanned out enough concurrent yt-dlp subprocesses (~73 MB each) to OOM the 256 MB prod VM. Resolved with a global 2-permit semaphore acquiring before the timeout clock starts, bounding the wait to load-shed rather than park; timed-out processes are now killed, not orphaned. Five duplicated invocation bodies folded into one capped helper.
